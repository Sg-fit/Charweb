INTRODUCTION

FRC Team 9094, the EarthQuakers, is the FIRST Robotics Competition team of Friends Central School, founded in 2022. The team's identity fuses the school's Quaker roots with the energy of competitive robotics — a gear-shaped emblem framing the Philadelphia skyline, cracked by jagged seismic lines in navy and green, capturing both where the team comes from and the disruptive energy it brings to the field.

In just three seasons, the EarthQuakers have competed in four full FRC game cycles — Charged Up, Crescendo, Reefscape, and Rebuilt — and qualified for the FIRST Championship (Worlds), the highest level of competition in the program.
